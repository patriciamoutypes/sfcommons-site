Rent The Commons

Host your next salon, event, talk, party, reception, hackathon, fireside chat, intimate gathering, or workshop at The Commons.

We work with individuals, small groups, organizations, and companies and can accommodate a wide-range of hosting needs.

Our spaces come with full AV and sound, tables and chairs, whiteboards, snacks & beverages, dedicated staff, projection capabilities, and tear-down + set-up services.
‍
We are in the heart of Hayes Valley (550 & 540 Laguna St.), steps away from Patricia's Green and premier eateries.
