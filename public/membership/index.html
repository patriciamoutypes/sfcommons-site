IRL & URL Community

Member-only Events

We've held over 800+ member-only events ranging co-created by the community from circles, salons, book clubs, lectures, hackathons, field trips, creative nights, discussion & debate groups, & more.

Host your events

Members can host events for free or at a heavily discounted rate for rentals. Many members leverage the space & community to experiment with new vocations, ways of being, and the art of facilitation.

Our Community

A home for the bold, curious, courageous, and earnest. We’re life-long learners, explorers of the human terrain, and truth-seekers of the intellect and heart.

Most concretely, we are a place where people are exploring new modes of being with self, others, and the world – and we invite every adventurer who is excited to be in the questions of who they are, and how they want to be in the world.
