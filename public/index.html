About

The Commons is a fourth place to deeply explore ourselves and the world, together. 


Our mission is to support people in the process of becoming more fully themselves, rooted in the belief that community is essential to that journey. And to do that in a space infused with playfulness, aliveness, agency, earnestness, abundance, and kindness.

FAQ

How much does a membership cost?



Here are our current prices for membership:
‍
• $190/month (monthly)
• $150/month (quarterly)

We also offer scholarships for those with significant financial hardship.
The Commons Membership is a way for us to maintain The Commons as a sacred space for the community, by the community. Memberships are the primary way we financially support The Commons which operates as a bootstrapped small-medium business. It ensures we don’t have to take VC funding or rent out the space to corporate or third parties who don’t share our values.

What's the status of my application?

We review applications on a rolling basis and accept new members on a bi-weekly basis.
‍
If you haven't heard back from us yet your application is still being considered and/or on a waiting list ordered by the time in which we've received your application.

Can I check-out the space & events as a non-member?

Feel free to check out our public calendar for events that are open to the public. Aside from that, the space is member only 7 days a week from 9am-10pm.

Do you sell day passes?

Yes! You can spend a full day at The Commons for $30. Choose your date, purchase your pass, and we’ll email you a QR code for door access.

Your pass is valid from 9:00 AM–10:00 PM on the day you select.

Purchase a Day Pass →

What type of programming do you have? 

We design our community and programming to holistically engage the mind, body, heart, and soul.
‍
• Community-wide gatherings to celebrate and commune together
• Intimate clubs to explore and meaning-make
• Unstructured time for deep work, hanging, and serendipitous encounters 
• Community-created and expert-led event programming 

Our programming includes salons, support circles, book clubs, workshops, and speaker events specifically designed to help us:  
‍
• Discover: explore life’s bigger picture and questions 
• Heal: understand, resolve, and integrate our past 
• Expand: take on new and diverse perspectives 
• Actualize: understand the world and our unique purpose within it 

For more insight on specific events, we frequently share them on our Twitter.

How can I host an event at The Commons?

If you're interested in a private rental that is not open to the public and Commons members you can submit your interest in our rentals page.

If you're interested in hosting a recurring event consider joining The Commons Gatherers Program or if you're more interested in hosting a 1-time event, reach out to us hello@thesfcommons.com

Are you dog and kid friendly?

Yes! We bring our whole selves to this space - which includes our furry companions and beloved kids. The space features an enclosed play pen area for kids.

For dogs, we ask that you keep them on leash as to not disturb other members. We welcome you to bring dog beds to ensure their comfort!
