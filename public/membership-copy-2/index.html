🏛️ Campus

Campus is at the core of what it means to be part of the Commons Membership Community.  It's our take on bringing the best parts of college into adulthood. A "school" for the soul, intellect, and inner-child. Every quarter, the community creates a curriculum and participates in a rotating set of circles, salons, juntos and extracurriculars. At the end of the quarter, we gather and celebrate our learnings at "nerd prom".

Join The Commons

Pricing Philosophy

The Commons Membership is a way for us to maintain The Commons as a sacred space for the community, by the community. Memberships are the primary way we financially support The Commons which operates as a bootstrapped small-medium business. It ensures we don’t have to take VC funding or rent out the space to corporate or third parties who don’t share our values. As a community-supported space, we operate on a model of financial transparency with our member community.
‍
Total Operational Costs: $5.5K/month
• Rent + Utilities: ~$16K/month
• Cleaning & Maintenance: ~$2K/month
• Tea & Coffee Bar: ~$1K/month
• Community Programming: ~$4K/month
• Misc Startup & Software Expenses: ~$1.5K/month
• Labor & Staffing Expenses: ~$35K/month
‍
