💌 👋 Hey there!

We're excited to extend an invite for you to join The Commons!  

The Commons is a “fourth place” dedicated to meaning-making, personal development, and self-expression in community. Our mission & vision is to fill the gap left by civic, social, and religious institutions of the past, for our current generation to come together in our curiosities, ask questions “of ultimate concern”, and show up more authentically in our lives.
‍
The Member Community is our way of making The Commons not only a space, but also a home: a tight-knit community for the curious, creative, and playful to explore & grow together.

We'd love for you to join because we thought you deeply embodied our values: holistic curiosity, kindness & playfulness, authentic agency, an abundance mindset, & deep earnestness. We hope you'll join us in calling The Commons a second home 🏠

Read more about the member experience and register below. Let us know if you have any questions at hello@thesfcommons.com

Pricing Philosophy

The Commons Membership is a way for us to maintain The Commons as a sacred space for the community, by the community. Memberships are the primary way we financially support The Commons which operates as a bootstrapped small-medium business. It ensures we don’t have to take VC funding or rent out the space to corporate or third parties who don’t share our values. As a community-supported space, we operate on a model of financial transparency with our member community.
‍
Total Operational Costs: $55.5K/month
• Rent + Utilities: ~$16K/month
• Cleaning & Maintenance: ~$2K/month
• Tea & Coffee Bar: ~$1K/month
• Community Programming: ~$4K/month
• Misc Startup & Software Expenses: ~$1.5K/month
• Labor & Staffing Expenses: ~$35K/month
