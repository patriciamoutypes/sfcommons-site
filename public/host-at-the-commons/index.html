The Gatherer Experience

01. COMMONS MEMBERSHIP INCLUDED

Your Gatherer membership includes the same access as a Commons member, so you can co-work, attend member events, use the space, and become part of the everyday life of the community, not just show up when you’re hosting.

02. Included Hosting Access

Apply to host recurring gatherings at The Commons without paying standard rental rates. Best suited for monthly or biweekly gatherings.

03. Built-In Community

‍Reach Commons members and friends who are already looking for thoughtful events, clubs, workshops, and spaces to connect.

04. Marketing Support

Gatherer events can be listed on our calendar and included in our regular event promotion flow across member channels.

05. A Beautiful Hosting Environment

Use our cozy rooms, tea station, cushions, chairs, tables, books, art supplies, ritual materials, and warm Commons atmosphere.

06. Peer Circle of Hosts

Come together with other Gatherers to share what you’re building, ask for support, trade notes, find collaborators, and shape the future of hosting at The Commons.

FAQ

Is this the same as renting the space?

No. Rentals are for one-off private events, offsites, parties, or organizations that simply need a venue. Gatherers is for recurring hosts who want to become part of The Commons ecosystem.

What if I'm interested in hosting a gathering, but not in the Gatherers program?

Yes. If you’re interested in hosting something at The Commons but don’t want to become a Gatherer, we may still be able to support you through a one-off gathering. Email us hello@thesfcommons.com.

Gatherers is best for people who want to host recurring clubs, circles, salons, workshops, or community-led events and become more integrated into The Commons ecosystem.

One-off gatherings are best for hosts who want to bring a thoughtful event into the space once, or test something before deciding whether it should become recurring.Rentals are best for private events, offsites, parties, company gatherings, or groups that primarily need a beautiful space without joining the Commons community or programming ecosystem.

If you’re not sure which path is right, reach out and we’ll help route you.

How often can I host?

Gatherers can request recurring hosting slots, usually monthly or biweekly, based on availability. We’ll work with accepted Gatherers to find the right rhythm.

Who is a good fit for Gatherers?

Club organizers, facilitators, artists, teachers, ritual hosts, movement guides, reading group leaders, salon hosts, community builders, and anyone who regularly brings people together around a shared curiosity, practice, or question.

Can I host paid events?

Yes. Paid events are allowed, but they may have a lower member-only commission or revenue share depending on the format and size of the event.

What kinds of events are not a fit?

Purely transactional events, hard-sell workshops, private parties, events that don’t align with the Commons ethos, or gatherings that require heavy operational support beyond what the program provides.
